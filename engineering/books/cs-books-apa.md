# Programming Fundamentals & Algorithms — Book List (APA 7th ed.)

A curated list of books for students learning programming fundamentals and algorithms.
Primarily Java-focused and language-agnostic titles.

References are formatted in APA 7th edition style and alphabetized by author surname
within each section. Retailer (Amazon) links are retained as requested; APA would
normally omit them, since they point to a sales page rather than to the work itself.

---

## Main book

Sedgewick, R., & Wayne, K. (2011). *Algorithms* (4th ed.). Addison-Wesley. https://www.amazon.com/dp/032157351X
Sedgewick, R., & Wayne, K. (2011). *Algorithms* (4th ed.) [Online materials]. Addison-Wesley. https://algs4.cs.princeton.edu/home/
